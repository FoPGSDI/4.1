# Appendix: Results for various Equations of State

This appendix  provides the numerical data for mode-frequencies of 12
realistic EOS. This data was used to infer the empirical relations
discussed in the main body of the paper.
We provide the data in the form of one table for each EOS.
In each table we list the central density, the radius and the mass of the
stellar model and the frequencies and the damping times
of the $f$-mode, the first $p$-mode and the first $w$-mode.
